Method notes

Each financing is joined to its collateral's PLUTO record(s). Split notes and pari passu pieces are de-duplicated; blanket loans repeated across condo parcels are counted once. Office uses PLUTO BldgArea as the denominator, multifamily uses UnitsRes. Modelled maturities are presented as estimates, never as fact; stated maturities are labelled stated when they appear.
