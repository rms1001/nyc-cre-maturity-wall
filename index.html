Why the full ratios remain open

Each loan must be joined to its collateral BBLs. Split notes and pari passu pieces must be de-duplicated. Blanket loans must not be repeated across every parcel. Office uses PLUTO BldgArea as requested, with OfficeArea as a sensitivity. Multifamily should use UnitsRes, while UnitsTotal stays visible for review. Condo billing lots, multi-building lots, conversions and property-type conflicts need manual review.
